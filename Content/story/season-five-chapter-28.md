### L28.S5 · plot beat · Rose, Quill
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE BOOKKEEPING. The war's costs lie entered; the manner of their keeping is the mistress's to choose. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The pages lie open — Brighton counted, the ball priced, the commission's ruin entered under malice — and Quill looks up at last, and the look is the question. How shall the war be kept? A book can be a burden, a weapon, or a witness. The keeping is hers to choose.)*
> *(Three ways to keep a war, and the pen is wet, and the morning waits on her word. Keep it as the war's cost — charged against the future, every figure a debt the years must repay. Keep it as the work's cost — the price of power, spent to hold what was won. Or keep it as the people's cost — every name entered beside every figure, the human measure in the margins. Each keeps the same figures. Each keeps a different war.)*
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — The bookkeeping:*
- **The war's cost** — *The future, charged.* Every figure a debt the coming years must repay; the war kept as burden, the accounts as obligation.
- **The work's cost** — *Power, priced.* The price of holding what was won; the war kept as investment, the accounts as strategy.
- **The people's cost** — *Every name, entered.* The human measure in the margins beside every figure; the war kept as witness, the accounts as memory.
> *(The game remembers the choice — and the means: the keeping, the tone, the ledger's reading. The destination was never in doubt. The signature is hers.)*
