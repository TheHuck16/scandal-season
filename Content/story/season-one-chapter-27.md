## Chapter 27 — "The Trap Rout"

**Occasion:** Rose's own rout — the whole ton in Augusta's drawing rooms, and one guest who must walk into the trap. **Preparation ritual:** the trap-rout gown — the hostess, dressed for command. **Purse shelf:** a rout's costs — candles, cards, and courage; the most expensive evening of the season, and worth every coin.

**What changes for Rose:** She hosts her first rout — her rooms, her rules — and each gentleman offers to stand with her publicly at the Assembly. She chooses one public ally, decides Octavia's place in the war, and sets the trap's spring: tonight, or the Assembly. Rackham walks in. Cecilia plays her last card. The Assembly, tomorrow — everything, wagered.
