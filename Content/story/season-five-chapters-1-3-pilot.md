**Canon applied:** 1815. The Network's London face named in S4 and known, unfallen; the war open. Lady Agatha Drummond leads the establishment's counterattack — social and financial pressure, never a press bid. The second installment is £5,000, called where everyone hears. Lucien de Valcourt never appears in person — only money and paper act. Mrs. Nance Bell is the Gazette's publicly named proprietor — soft-voiced, sharp-eyed; the paper judges and narrates, never a mystery, never a prize. Fashion is power, worn deliberately. Energy is the only throttle. Iron rule holds: time earns everything money can. Crowns (premium) / coins (soft). No Crown SKU touches the ritual.

**Occasion:** Augusta's at-home, an evening; the ton gathers; Drummond expected, and pleasant. **Preparation ritual:** the evening gown for being named a debtor in public — severity, cut close; the gown answers before the girl does. **Purse shelf:** the London household at ninety pounds a month; the Park works at two hundred a quarter; the held installment, waiting like weather.

**What changes for Rose:** The second installment is named where everyone hears — £5,000, in public, among friends — and Rose answers before the gasp, standing. The ton learns the terms of her debt and the terms of her nerve together. Drummond's pleasantness is the worst of it, and the war with the establishment is now open.
