## Chapter 1 — "The Ledger"

**Occasion:** Bath; the estate accounts laid bare at Augusta's Bath house. **Preparation ritual:** the London-departure dress — dressing for the money world. **Purse shelf:** £17,100 in hand against £50,000 known.

**What changes for Rose:** She learns the true size of the Hartwell paper Rackham scattered across England — £58,000 named, a £32,900 gap she cannot economize away — and resolves to hunt the paper holder by holder rather than beg for it. The money is in London. She will go where the money is.

### L1.S5 · plot beat · Rose, Augusta, Letitia, Fenwick
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE LEDGER'S ANSWER (the estate's arithmetic, and the only road through it). (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The library holds its breath. Four people and fifty-eight thousand pounds of bad news, and the question on the table is the oldest one in the book: what does the heir do? Augusta waits the way she does everything — as though the answer were already decided and she were merely curious which one Rose would pick.)*
> *(The destination is fixed: the restoration must be finished, the paper must be answered, and the Hartwell name must come out of Rackham's shadow clean. The means are hers to choose. Price the three roads: the hunt, the borrowing, or the belt pulled tight.)*
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — The ledger's answer:*
- **Hunt the paper** — *Resolve+, the hunter's road.* Buy it back holder by holder, at whatever discount honor allows — the paper is the fortune's shadow, and shadows can be bought.
Rose looks at the schedule of holders and sees not a burden but a map.
- **Raise City credit** — *Daring+, the borrower's road.* Go to London and borrow against the estate's future — the money world lends to those who look like they will win.
Augusta's eyebrow rises a fraction; she has borrowed in her time, and remembers the taste.
- **Retrench and save** — *Prudence+, the miser's road.* Halt the works, live small, let the rents accumulate — the slow road, the safe road, the turnip road.
Letitia sets down her chocolate as though it had disappointed her personally.
> *(The books close with a sound like a door shutting. Whatever road she chose, the library understands: the staring is over. What follows is arithmetic with intent.)*
> *(The game remembers the choice — and the means: the hunt, the borrowing, or the belt pulled tight. The destination was never in doubt. The signature is hers.)*
