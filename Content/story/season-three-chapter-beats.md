# Scandal Season — Season Three Chapter Beats

**STATUS: DRAFT** · September 27, 2026
**Sources:** `ten-season-storyboard-beats.md` ("Season 3 — The Libel"), `ten-season-plot-arc.md` (S3 section), `build-handoff/story/book-one-arc.md` (S3 — canonical), `content/characters.json` (canon names). Format mirrors `build-handoff/story/season-two-chapter-beats.md`.
**Season Three title (locked):** "The Libel"
**Story time:** roughly ten days, Bath → London.
**Engine:** The Network's gutter weapon — Ambrose Pyke's *London Lantern* runs a libel campaign against Rose (the ruin retold as family fraud, the suitors as dupes, the restoration as swindle). Rose must win in the open, because she cannot own the judge: the Gazette is not for sale — the Network leans on Bell (pressure, money, menaces) and Bell refuses in ONE scene, soft-voiced, absolute — character, not plot. Never an authorship plot.
**Anchor:** the gutter (the libel war, fought in the open) + the stables (Rose rides the courier lines — paper suppliers, compositors, hawkers, press chapels — learning how libels travel the way she learned how money travels).
**Decision rule (LOCKED Sep 27, 2026):** EXACTLY 3 key decisions per chapter — 90 for the season. Decision points below are attached to the chapters they resolve, not scenes themselves.
**Event anchor (content calendar, NOT a chapter):** a riding week (equestrian season event pass) — scheduled by the content calendar; it does not occupy a chapter.
**Ritual rule (standing):** one dressing-ritual [C] placement per chapter below — placement only; full ritual detailing comes later. No Crown SKUs touch rituals; the jewel shelf stays open, never a ritual step.

## Canon notes (non-negotiable)

- Year 1815. **Lucien de Valcourt does NOT appear in S3.** The Network's coin may be traced to its purse, but his face stays offstage — S4 is his confrontation. Never promote him; the purse is proved, the face withheld.
- Mrs. Nance Bell is the Gazette's publicly named proprietor from the first edition — no unmasking, hunt, or control-of-the-paper plot anywhere. The Network's lean on Bell (Ch 20) is ONE scene — pressure, money, menaces, her mother's dismissed-housekeeper past thrown as the handle — and her refusal is soft-voiced, absolute: character, not plot. It never works.
- Suitors honorable unless genuinely villainous; villains sly, never overt. Pyke never threatens directly — he prints. Each suitor keeps a secret about the Network in S3; trust strains by increments, slow and honorable.
- Lavinia Crane is Rose's ally with her own agenda: the *Lantern* once printed filth about her family; she wants Pyke BROKEN, not bought. Never buying — the verdict is won in the open.
- Pyke exits at season's end — one season, spent, final. The gutter-press machine he embodied remains a reusable story tool for later seasons.
- Cecilia: S3 texture only (the long absence continues; S4–S7 absent). Drummond: absent S3–S4.
- Octavia's sitters: per the Sep 27, 2026 resolution — the Network's money men, polite émigré financiers paying in coin; she never knew whose faces she was painting.
- Floriography (S3): **white roses of the clean name.**
- Continuity: S2 Ch30 ended with the *Lantern's* libel PRINTED and Rose's line "Let them print." S3 Ch1 opens from that cliffhanger.
- Working names for minor gutter functionaries (detailing phase to confirm): **Mr. Creech** (paper supplier), **Ned Larkin** (hawker), **Mr. Pettifer** (chapel father of the *Lantern's* press).

## Beat-type legend

Same vocabulary as the S2 inventory: **Dialogue-heavy [D]** — two people talking until something true slips out. **Fashion-selection [F]** — styling as courtship strategy with social consequence. **Dressing-for-climax [C]** — the ritual of preparing to be seen (one per chapter). **Texture/micro-beat [T]** — seconds of play. **Plot beat** — story beats. **Plot beat (villain)** / **Plot beat (romance: route)** — tagged threads. **Gazette sting** — the chapter's closing paper verdict. **Cliffhanger** — the chapter's closing beat. Every beat carries real stakes for Rose.

## Animation legend

Same convention as S1/S2: **Shared** — reusable shared animation set. **Custom** — bespoke animation for tentpole set-pieces, labeled with a reason. Production judgment, to be validated by the animation lead.
