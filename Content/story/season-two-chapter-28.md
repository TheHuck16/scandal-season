### L28.S7 · plot beat · Rose Hartwell, Augusta
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE MISSING PAPER. The strategy: what to do about the hole in the pattern. (Turns: 3 — the decision itself)*
> "The missing page," Rose said. "Quill's file is complete but for one paper. Taken, or never there. I must decide what to do about the hole before the war decides for me."
> Augusta regarded her over the teacup. "Three roads, as I see it. Walk one."
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — The missing paper:*
- **Go after it now** — *The hunt begins at once.* Rose tasks Quill and the hall to hunt the missing paper before the Network's next move; the war's tempo is hers — but the hunt spends the season's hard-won quiet, and a hunter is easier to ambush than a household at rest.
- **Let S3's war come first** — *Patience as strategy.* Rose holds her position and lets the Network move first; the gutter will answer Bath, and the answer will show where the paper went — but waiting cedes the initiative, and the principal may use the page before she finds it.
- **Assume the principal holds it** — *Plan around the worst.* Rose proceeds as if Lucien de Valcourt himself holds the missing page, and shapes every move accordingly — the safest assumption, and the one that costs the most sleep.
> Rose weighed the three roads the way the season had taught her — by profit and by price. The hunt at once: tempo hers, quiet spent. Patience: the gutter answering, initiative ceded. The worst assumed: every move shaped around it, sleep spent instead. Three roads. "Choose," said Augusta, "and I shall back the choice. That is what rear guards are for."
> Rose chose — and Augusta nodded once, slowly, the way a general acknowledges an order she would have given herself, and poured more tea, which was the Dowager's whole vocabulary of approval.
