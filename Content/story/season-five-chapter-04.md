**Turns.** Every ordinary scene carries 7–10 numbered player turns `(T# · type)` inline: look closer, stance, tone, choice, social maneuver, dialogue, or remembered micro-decision, each closed with a remembered note. Outcomes converge: the game remembers the means (tone, relationship movement, Gazette flavor), never as a plot branch. ★ key-decision scenes carry the decision's 3 options as their turns; the Gazette sting is a single reading turn. The [C] ritual's direction + twenty 5-coin pins are its 21 turns; the ritual runs long and sits outside the 350–450-word scene budget.

**Canon applied:** 1815. The road from Brighton back to Hartwell Park; the tenant cottages, cottage by cottage: the roofs, the rents, the people. The steward's plain question answered in person. Augusta's "mind the ground," kept. The dragon sails Thursday and follows, learning at last what ground means. The second installment stands at £5,000; Quill's plan holds. Lucien de Valcourt never appears in person. Mrs. Nance Bell judges and narrates, soft-voiced, sharp-eyed. Energy is the only throttle. Iron rule holds: time earns everything money can. No Crown SKU touches the ritual.

**Occasion:** the cottages' week: the roofs, the rents, the people; the land, seen with her own eyes. **Preparation ritual:** the tenants' supper gown: to be seen by the people, not the ton; land, worn honestly. **Purse shelf:** the roofs, costed at the thatcher's rate; the rents, the arrears, carried or forgiven; the £5,000, assembling on her day, not the dragon's.

**What changes for Rose:** She goes home and sees, cottage by cottage, whose lives her power touches — the roofs, the rents, the people, the steward's plain question answered in person. The dragon follows her to the country and is shown the ground, and learns at last what the word means. The glitter answers to the land. The land does not answer to the glitter.
