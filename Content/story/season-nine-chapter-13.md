### L13.S9 · plot beat · Rose (alone) — "The Answer, Owed"
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE ANSWER (accept the last de Varenne money, refuse it, or delay the answer). The offer is weighed; decide before the week decides for her. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The library, Monday night: the offer weighed whole — the last de Varenne money, the family's final reserve, the final illusion laid at her feet without conditions. *★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — The Answer:*
- **Accept** — *Take the last de Varenne money.* The rescue accepted: the defense funded — counsel's fees, the paper hunt, the hearing's costs. The ledger gains its new line, honestly come by. But a taken rescue is a debt of the heart, and debts of the heart keep their own books, and the ton will price the taking in the town's grammar.
- **Refuse** — *Send it back whole.* The pride kept, the defense unhelped, the illusion left in his keeping. The house stands on its own: nothing owed, nothing taken, the conscience clear. But pride does not pay counsel, and the week is expensive, and the hearing will not wait while pride counts its change.
- **Delay** — *Hold the answer.* The week turns, the knowledge fills, the money waits. Decided from fuller knowledge, the answer ripens. But a held answer is a held man, and Laurent waits at four o'clocks that do not come, and a Frenchman held too long stops waiting and starts deciding.
> *(Taken, returned, or held — the night will sign one of them, and the carrying will be plain. The money was always going to be answered. The signature is hers.)*
