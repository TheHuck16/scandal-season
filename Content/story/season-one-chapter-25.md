## Chapter 25 — "The Refuge Picnic"

**Occasion:** A borrowed afternoon in Octavia's walled garden; the war, set down for three hours. **Preparation ritual:** the refuge dress — country, honest, wind-proof. **Purse shelf:** the picnic costs nothing; the refuge is beyond price.

**What changes for Rose:** She hears Julian's confession on paper, chooses what to do with the man and the channel — and in Octavia's garden decides what the one free friendship of her season is allowed to become. Rackham's last pressure and Cecilia's desperation arrive at the garden gate. The lock-in: the route chosen, the channel ready, the war's end in sight.
