**Canon applied:** 1815. The second installment stands at £5,000, assembling on its Tuesday, at noon, at the London house. Lucien de Valcourt never appears in person; only his money and paper act. Lady Agatha Drummond is answered only for now; she returns, unbowed and unbroken. Mrs. Nance Bell judges and narrates, soft-voiced, sharp-eyed, blunt. The gutter sheet is unnamed. Energy is the only throttle. Iron rule holds: time earns everything money can. No Crown SKU touches the ritual.

**Occasion:** the yacht week — the Pavilion set afloat; the fleet, the wagers, the sea stage; pleasure as the arena. **Preparation ritual:** the fleet's dinner, afloat — the armor, dressed for the water; pleasure, stitched for the sea. **Purse shelf:** the week's small costs, honestly paid; the £5,000, assembling on its Tuesday, untouched; the yacht's hire, the dinners, the wagers, the steward's figures.

**What changes for Rose:** She sails the yacht week as its host and its wager, pleasure fully armored, and the sabotage widens under the glitter: a second gown shows the brown bleed, the Thursday fitting fails, and the atelier's war comes home to the water. The banking houses ask their questions and get their answers in coin; the dragon's courting of Lavinia's circle deepens; and the week afloat gets its first entry in the other ledger, the one the sea never reads.
