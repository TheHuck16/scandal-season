## Chapter 16 — "The Contradiction"

**Occasion:** The daily drives with the Comte de Varenne; Augusta's staged courtship. **Preparation ritual:** the drive dress — composure at twenty yards (the Contradiction Gown). **Purse shelf:** the drives cost little; the proof costs sleep.

**What changes for Rose:** She holds written proof that the Hartwell ruin was manufactured — and learns that proof and the ton's story are two different currencies, spent in different rooms. Augusta's staged courtship begins to chafe; the tired man underneath Laurent's manners is seen fully for the first time; Octavia's studio becomes the one unperformed room in London — and Cecilia walks into it.
