> *(Dawn, the still-room: the maid, waiting — white-faced no longer, but urgent, alive with the importance of it.)*
> "Miss." — the maid (low, fast) "Last night — after — one of the laundry girls heard him. Rackham's man. He wasn't alone. There was another — a gentleman, miss, by his voice — and he said —" (she swallows) "— he said the next drop isn't Tuesday. It's *tonight*. A name, a place —" (the whisper, barely breath:) *"— the Blue Boar, at ten. Ask for —"*
> *(The whisper lands. A name. A place. A drop — tonight. The Tuesday basket was never the whole of it. And somewhere below stairs, the house holds its breath with her.)*
