### L30.S1 · [T] · Rose (alone)
*Purpose: the legacy's turning — the Hartwell's, the whole; the years, the kept. (Turns: 7)*
> *(A week after the Assembly, Rose walks the Park at dawn — the first solitary hour she has had since the invitations went out.)*
> *(The house has had seven days to become itself again. The last carriage went down the drive a week ago, the last lamp with it, and since then the shutters have opened on their own hinges, the fires have been lit for nobody in particular, and the chocolate comes at eight because it is eight, not because anyone is watching. The Assembly is over. The annual has begun.)*
> *(The east terrace first — dew on the stone, the urns with their autumn planting — then down the long lawn to the lake, where the mist is thick enough to hide the far bank. The birds are at it: pheasants in the coverts, ducks on the water, a heron standing in the shallows like a grey opinion. > *(The lawns run clean to the lake. The hedges stand trimmed within an inch. The drive's gravel is raked, though no carriage will come today to admire it. The walled garden — the restoration's longest labor, reclaimed across the seasons — holds its autumn in good order. Ten seasons ago this was a ruin with a view. The view remains. The ruin does not.)*
> *(She takes the long way: past the lake, where the mist still sits on the water; past the new hedges, where the drainage runs true; past the tenants' cottages, where the chimneys are already smoking. The village is awake. The estate is working. Nobody requires anything of her before breakfast, which feels — she checks the feeling, and keeps it — like wealth.)*
> *(At the lake's edge she stops. Ten seasons behind her, the annual years ahead, the mist burning off the water like a curtain going up on nothing in particular. She does not make a speech. The Park has never asked for one.)*

### L30.S4 · [T] · Rose (alone)
*Purpose: the years — the legacy's; the kept, the whole. (Turns: 7)*
> *(Ten seasons, written in the ground — the Park is the diary, kept in earth and water instead of ink, and every page is legible to anyone who walks it.)*
> *(The first season's page: the lake choked with weed, the hedges grown wild into the walks, the walled garden a bramble wilderness, the roof letting in the sky. The ruin was honest — no pretense, no repair — and she walked it with Quill's inventory in her hand, counting what was left. What was left was the ground, and the name, and the stubbornness. It was enough to start with. It had to be.)*
> *(The middle seasons' pages: the lake dredged, the hedges cut back to their lines, the garden's walls rebuilt stone by stone, the drainage laid, the planting begun. The years of mud and money — Harrow's ledgers, the bailiff's reports, the oaks chosen over the timber cash. The record is underfoot: the mud, the money, the choosing.)*
> *(The later pages, written fair: the hedges trimmed within an inch, the garden walled and ordered, the Hartwell rose bred from a hundred seedlings. The diary's last pages, and the hand is confident now, the entries short. Finished things do not need long entries.)*
> *(She reads it as she walks. Here the fence she mended herself in the second season, because the carpenter was laid up and the fence would not wait. Here the bed she planted in the fourth, getting the spacing wrong and being told so by the gardener at length. Here the view she cleared in the seventh, standing in the mud in her good boots and not caring. The years are not behind her. They are under her feet, which is the more reliable place for them.)*
> *(At the walled garden's gate she stops. The ruin, the labor, the finishing — the record is complete, and she does not write it in the household book, because the Park has already written it, better than ink, where anyone can read it who bothers to walk.)*

### L30.S9 · ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 · Rose (alone)
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE LEGACY'S LAW (what the legacy's law is: the whole, the keeping, or the held). The years deepen; decide the law. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The years deepen, and the legacy asks its law. Rose stands at the lake's edge — the water, the mist burned off now, the swans at their stations — and understands that the legacy has three possible laws, and she must choose which one governs.)*
> *(The whole: the Park's law. The legacy governed by the whole means the ground is the argument — the hedges, the lake, the garden, the ten seasons made visible in earth and water, judged by what the eye can see. The keeping: the household's law. The legacy governed by the keeping means the practice is the point — the books, the debts, the mornings, the promise carried forward daily. The held: the record's law. The legacy governed by the held means the paper is the seal — the charter, the Gazette, the verdict set down in ink and kept.)*
> *(Three laws, and all of them true — that is the difficulty. The whole is hers; she built it. The keeping is the work; she does it. The held is the proof; she has it. She holds the three up the way she held the Assembly's guest list a year ago, looking for the flaw that would decide her. There is no flaw. There is only the choosing, and the choosing is the law.)*
> *(The bailiff rides the boundary in the distance, a small figure against the hedges. The village chimneys smoke. The Park goes about its business, indifferent to laws — which, Rose thinks, is the Park's own law, and no help at all. The law will be read in everything the legacy does from here, the way the ton reads everything. She had better choose well, and then — the harder part — live inside the choosing.)*
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — The legacy's law:*
- **The whole** — *the Park, lawed.* The law is the ground: the Park kept whole, the ten seasons argued in hedges and water. (Consequence: the legacy will be judged by the ground — what the Park shows, the years must answer for.)
- **The keeping** — *the held, lawed.* The law is the practice: the household's daily work, the books, the promise carried forward. (Consequence: the legacy will be judged by the keeping — the mornings, the ledgers, the ordinary resumed.)
- **The held** — *the kept, lawed.* The law is the record: the charter, the Gazette, the verdict set down. (Consequence: the legacy will be judged by the record — what is written, witnessed, and kept in ink.)
> *(She does not decide it standing at the lake — the law wants its proper hour, and the hour is not yet. The swans glide. The bailiff rides on. The water moves, and the law waits, undecided, the way the best things wait: without hurry. Whichever she chooses, the choosing is the point, and the years will do the rest.)*
