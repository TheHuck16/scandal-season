Fashion is a courtship mechanic with social consequence. Energy is the only throttle. Iron rule holds: time earns everything money can. Crowns (premium) / coins (soft).

**What changes for Rose:** She drives out with Laurent and hears the émigré whispers about Rackham — the ruin's shape, spoken in a French accent over carriage wheels — and asks herself, in traffic, whether she is being courted or managed; Julian's note, knowing her calendar, joins the drawer.
