## Chapter 17 — "The Rout"

**Occasion:** The Honorable Mrs. Trent's rout — the crush: three hundred people in rooms built for eighty. **Preparation ritual:** the rout gown — the allegiance made in advance, with pins. **Purse shelf:** the rout's tickets and tokens.

**What changes for Rose:** She declares an allegiance in pins before she ever enters the room — gold for Laurent, the scarlet ribbon for Julian, the severe for Henry — and then dances the declaration in front of three hundred witnesses. Rackham's men watch the doors; Cecilia's interest fixes on the miniature; and Octavia's warning, given between dances, names what the war is now about.
