## Occasion
Friday to Wednesday; the drawing rooms; the counter.

**Preparation ritual:** the drawing-room armor — dressed for the calls. **Purse shelf:** the day's instruments — the household book, the visiting book, the memorandum book; the card case, full.

## What changes for Rose
Cecilia turns the drawing rooms one by one, the flattery tailored and years-measured. The Trelawneys waver; Agatha Drummond holds, to see everything. Augusta deploys the counter-campaign — truth, door to door, not flattery. Rose chooses how to meet the turning, how to answer the waverers, and what part to take in the counter. Bell prints the held. The rooms' week ends with the week's accounting honest — and the Lash's planted story fires the print war's first shot.
