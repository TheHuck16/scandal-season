## Chapter 26 — "Quill's Strongbox"

**Occasion:** The solicitor's chambers; the counting of paper. **Preparation ritual:** the chambers dress — gravity, authority, a woman who means to be believed. **Purse shelf:** coins for clerks' fees and copying; the law's arithmetic, honestly paid.

**What changes for Rose:** The codicil, the schedules, and the auditors' findings converge in Quill's strongbox — the ruin, proved engineered, in one room. She decides what the evidence is for, which channel carries the final edition, and what the household is told. A copying clerk stands where no clerk should be; one schedule never arrived. Henry walks her home the long way and talks about nothing. The final edition — the trap's last piece — goes into type.
