# Scandal Season — Season One, Chapters 1–3: Full Scene Detail (FORMAT PILOT)

**STATUS: PILOT** — September 27, 2026. This document pilots the scene-detailing format for the 30-chapters × 40-scenes container. Review the format before scaling: per-scene type tags, characters, purpose, dialogue beats (Regency register), player choices, exactly-3 marked key decisions per chapter, occasion briefs at dressing rituals, Gazette stings in Bell's voice, cliffhangers, and per-scene animation calls.

**Sources:** `scandal-season-storyboard/season-one-storyboard-v0.2.md` (beat-level), `build-handoff/story/season-one-scenes.md` (beat inventory — built on, not duplicated), `character-roster.md` (canon names), `build-handoff/content/chapter-dressing.md` (locked ritual design).

**Format key.** [D] dialogue-heavy · [F] fashion-selection (2–3 diegetic options, no coin cost, remembered) · [C] dressing-for-climax ritual (full ritual: direction + occasion brief, decomposed micro-decisions, story morsels, reveal coda) · [T] texture micro-beat · plot beat (untagged) · Gazette sting · cliffhanger. ★ marks the chapter's exactly 3 key decisions (locked rule: 3/chapter, 90/season). Animation: Shared (reusable staging) or Custom (bespoke, reason given) — none of L1–L3 are tentpoles, so all calls here are Shared, including the shared ritual presentation.

**Canon applied:** 1815. Rose Hartwell rebuilds Hartwell Park and earns her place; unhurried about marriage, honorable in method. Suitors honorable; routes grow through friendship. Villains sly, never overt. Mrs. Nance Bell is the Gazette's publicly named proprietor from the first edition — no unmasking plot anywhere; the paper judges and narrates, never a mystery, prize, or paywall. Fashion is a courtship mechanic with social consequence. Energy is the only throttle. Iron rule holds: time earns everything money can. Crowns (premium) / coins (soft).

## Chapter 1 — "Ink Before Arrival"

**Occasion:** The road to London; arrival at Augusta's house. **Preparation ritual:** the travel-stained gown made presentable — Rose's first merge-board task: *restore, don't replace.* **Purse shelf:** threadbare but proud. Coin income: tutorial.

**What changes for Rose:** She arrives in London under her grandmother's campaign, the Season's terms set by her own tongue — and a forged engagement waiting in a solicitor's letter.
