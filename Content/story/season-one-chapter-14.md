**Chapter 14 · STATUS: FULL** — September 27, 2026. Full 40-scene detail for the 30-chapters × 40-scenes container. Format per `story/season-one-chapters-1-3-pilot.md`.

### L14.S11 · plot beat · Rose, the steward
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE STEWARD.*
> *(A frightened man, a locked room, papers that could hang a house. Rose must choose how to hold him.)*

- **Buy him** — *Coin cost; steward flag: bound.* A purse, plainly offered: "For your loyalty, which I am *purchasing* because I value it." *(He takes it; the transaction steadies them both.)*
