### L14.S8 · plot beat · Rose (alone) — "How to Refuse It"
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — HOW TO REFUSE (refuse kindly, refuse firmly, or refuse by giving him no ground to stand on). The offering honored; decide the manner before the morning decides it. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The library, Saturday night: the refusal formed whole — the commission unofferable-back, the resignation unsound three ways, the offering the point that must be honored. The firmly — refuse him outright, the Hartwell plainness, no softness for the whispers to misread, the kindest-as-firmest. The no-ground — refuse by giving him no ground to stand on: the case argued whole, the resignation made impossible before it is declined, Quill's three joints turned into a floor with no boards. Each road refuses the commission. Each refuses it differently, and the difference is the signature.)*
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — How to Refuse:*
- **Kindly** — *the gentle refusal.* She refuses him kindly — the friend's softness, the offering cradled even as it is declined, the manner Augusta named and the heart's first choice. (Consequence: he is honored in the refusing, the offering kept warm — but kindness gives the whispers softness to misread, and the town misreads kindly the way it misreads everything gentle.)
- **Firmly** — *the plain refusal.* She refuses him firmly — the Hartwell plainness, no softness for the whispers to misread, the kindest which is the firmest. (Consequence: nothing for the town to misread, the refusal clean as a parade ground — but firmness can bruise the offering, and the offering was the point that must not be bruised.)
- **No ground to stand on** — *the argued refusal.* She refuses by giving him no ground to stand on — the case argued whole, the resignation made impossible, then declined, Quill's counsel turned to architecture. (Consequence: he cannot resign what cannot be resigned, the floor gone before the fall — but an argued refusal leaves no room for the heart, and the heart made the offer and will feel the arguing.)
> *(Kindly, firmly, or no ground at all — the night will sign one of them, and the refusing will be shaped. The commission was always going to be refused. The signature is hers.)*
