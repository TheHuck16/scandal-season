# Scandal Season — Prose Standards

*Locked September 29, 2026. Mandatory first read for every chapter-writing brief before any scene is written.*

## The standing rule

**The prose and dialogue had to make sense to a human reader. Keep it fresh and like a human wrote it.**

New drafts must be human-clean from the first draft. No AI-tell cleanup passes afterward — if a draft needs one, the brief failed, not the writer. Regex sweeps never substitute for reading every sentence.

## Banned patterns

- **Generic AI diction.** No *delve*, *tapestry*, *vibrant*, *testament to*, *intricate*, *nestled*, *a symphony of*. If a word would never leave a real person's mouth in 1815, it doesn't leave yours.
- **The not-X-but-Y crutch.** No reversals of the form "it wasn't grief, it was arithmetic" / "not a ball, a battlefield." Say the true thing directly.
- **Abstraction triplets.** No "grief, duty, and the long arithmetic of loss." Two nouns are usually one too many; prefer one concrete noun.
- **Repetitive em-dash parentheticals.** Varied sentence lengths. Not every sentence gets an em-dash aside; most get none.
- **Thesis dialogue.** People don't speak in arguments. They deflect, they joke, they change the subject, they say the wrong thing and correct themselves.
- **Moralizing closers.** Never end a scene by summarizing its theme. End on an image, a line of dialogue, a gesture — something seen, not something concluded.
- **Doubled restatements.** Don't say it in the prose and then say it again in the turn line. The turn line advances; it doesn't echo.

## Required qualities

- **Varied sentence lengths.** Short ones land. Long ones carry. Never all of one.
- **Specific concrete period detail.** The weight of a reticule, the sound of a pen, the exact flower — not "the atmosphere."
- **Wry human dialogue.** Dry, oblique, period-textured. Letitia is funny without trying; Octavia is precise; Bell is blunt; Élise is warm.
- **Natural scene endings.** The scene stops because the moment stops, not because the point is made.

## Register

Wry, concrete, period-textured. Specific nouns over grand abstractions. Rose thinks in logistics and ledgers; she feels in images she would never say aloud.

## Canon discipline

- Named entities come from the source, never invented. When expanding, run a named-entity check against the brief — a fabricated surname once shipped as "Lavinia Marlowe" and had to be repaired.
- Money figures, once stated, never drift. (£41,600; £8,800 now; £3,000 a year for ten years; £2,800 to the de Valcourts promised; £150 to Harrow; £2,000 refused.)
- Flowers carry their locked meanings. Violets: faithfulness. White lilies: purity of intent. Rosemary: remembrance.
