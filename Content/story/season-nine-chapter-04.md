### L4.S8 · plot beat · Rose (alone) — "The Answer's Choosing"
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE ANSWER (how to answer his pity: accept it, deflect it, or return it in kind). The ball is Thursday; decide before the ball decides for her. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*

*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — The Answer:*
- **Accept it** — *Gracious, and taken.* She lets his pity stand — the gracious defendant, town sympathy taken where it falls. (Consequence: the sympathy flows her way, warm and freely given — but pity accepted is pity confirmed, and the confirmed are already half-defeated.)
- **Deflect it** — *Frameless, and refused.* She refuses pity's frame — every exchange about the papers, the case, the substance; pity given nowhere to land. (Consequence: the campaign stays on paper, where she is strongest — but deflection reads as coldness, and the town punishes cold women.)
- **Return it in kind** — *The pity-me-back.* She pities him in kind — for his father's story, his righteousness, his lonely crusade. (Consequence: the weapon turns, and the town delights in the symmetry — but pity given is pity owned, and the owner can be called cruel.)
