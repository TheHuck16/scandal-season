### L17.S6 · plot beat · Rose (alone) — "The Judgment, Weighed"
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — HOW TO JUDGE HIM (harshly, with mercy, or as a Hartwell judges). Choose the judgment before the week judges it. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The library at night. The question the dark keeps asking: how does she judge the grandfather? She prices the three roads at the window while the candles burn low. Harshly — the breaking faced in daylight, the irregularity owned in full, the law's word the last word. With mercy — the saving first, the estate kept standing, the man's reasons given their kindest true reading. As a Hartwell judges — the both held together, the breaking irregular and the saving true, the house's own measure.)*
> *(The library ledger habit does not stop at the dead. The harsh road buys clarity at the price of cold. The merciful road buys kindness at the price of softness. The Hartwell road buys evenness at the price of being hard to explain. The candles do not care which she chooses. The portraits, possibly, do.)*
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — How to Judge Him:*
- **Harshly** — *the plain, the daylight.* She judges him harshly — names the breaking plainly, in daylight, and lets the law's word stand as the last word. (Consequence: the exactness leaves the town's gossips nothing to work with — but daylight is a hard reader of the dead, and the town will read her severity before it reads her justice.)
- **With mercy** — *the kind, the saving-first.* She judges him with mercy — the saving first, the estate kept standing, the man's reasons given their kindest true reading. (Consequence: the kindness gives the dead their gentlest true reading — but mercy is a soft reader of paper, and the town will read the softness before it reads the truth.)
- **As a Hartwell judges** — *the both, the house's measure.* She judges him as a Hartwell judges — holds the both together, the breaking irregular and the saving true, the house's own measure. (Consequence: the dead are judged without cruelty and without softness — but evenness is the hardest road, and the town will want a simpler story.)
> *(She makes the choice before the candles burn down — whichever road it is, the handwriting will be hers. The grandfather was always going to be judged this week. The only question was in whose manner.)*
