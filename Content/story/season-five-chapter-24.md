Energy is the only throttle. Iron rule holds: time earns everything money can. No Crown SKU touches the ritual.

**Occasion:** Saturday — the Assembly's morning; the wearing, worn into the day. **Preparation ritual:** the Assembly gown — severe, magnificent, unmistakable; the single bloom. **Purse shelf:** the bloom, the gown, the morning's arithmetic; the open purse, the ready.

**What changes for Rose:** At dawn she chooses the single bloom from Élise's bouquet and pins it where the whole Assembly can see it. The household makes its last preparations, Augusta gives her final counsel, and Rose walks to the Assembly's doors with her terms already worn — the severe charcoal gown, the one flower, the decision made.
