**Canon applied:** 1815. Rose Hartwell rebuilds Hartwell Park and earns her place; unhurried about marriage, honorable in method. Suitors honorable; villains sly, never overt. Mrs. Nance Bell is the Gazette's publicly named proprietor from the first edition — no unmasking plot anywhere; the paper judges and narrates, never a mystery, prize, or paywall. Fashion is a courtship mechanic with social consequence. Energy is the only throttle. Iron rule holds: time earns everything money can. Crowns (premium) / coins (soft). No Crown SKU touches the ritual.

**Occasion:** Lavinia Crane's tea table — the ton's court in miniature. **Preparation ritual:** the tea gown — afternoon respectability with teeth. **Purse shelf:** the tea's small costs, honestly paid.

**What changes for Rose:** She holds Lavinia's court on her own terms — neither cut nor conquered — and finds her first allies among the wallflowers; Julian's hothouse rose arrives in public and complicates everything; the collection begins in secret, and Bell's Gazette turns its eye to her admirers.

*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — Lavinia's bait:*
- **Rise to it** — *Rivalry+.* "He mended a wheel, Miss Crane. I have seen dukes do less, usefully." *(The queens inhale; Lavinia's smile does not move.)*
- **Laugh it off** — *Lavinia disarmed, briefly.* "Economy is the season's fashion, Miss Crane. I am simply ahead of it." *(A laugh, surprised out of the circle.)*
- **Redirect to Cecilia** — *Cecilia+, ominous.* "Cecilia knows Lord Julian far better than I — she watched the whole rescue from her carriage. Didn't you, darling?" *(Cecilia's honey curdles by a degree.)*
