### L12.S4 · plot beat · Rose (alone) — "The Answer's Question"
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE ANSWER (accept, refuse, or delay). Eleven o'clock; the offer is priced before the morning prices it for her. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The morning room, eleven o'clock. Julian gone, Augusta's counsel filed, the lilies still white and still unimpressed. Rose takes out a scrap of paper and writes three words on it, the way she writes everything she means to be honest about: ACCEPT. REFUSE. DELAY. The ink is barely dry before the house starts offering opinions through the walls — a footman's step, quick; the post arriving below; Augusta's pen, audible through the ceiling. The week is listening. She prices the roads before the morning prices them for her.)*
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE ANSWER:*
- **Accept** — *Take the name.* She takes it — Julian's shield raised before the ton, a married woman's standing, the claimant's paper blunted against a wife. The ton, bored by respectability, looks elsewhere. (Consequence: the shield is real and the hearing is faced two abreast — but the ton will call it a rescue whatever she calls it, and gratitude is thin ground to build a marriage on.)
- **Refuse** — *Stand alone.* She declines the name and faces Tuesday in her own: upright, unshielded, the defendant's own name on the paper. Julian's offer stays clean of her refusal. (Consequence: every debt she owes herself is kept — but the ton's adjectives go unanswered, and unanswered adjectives have a way of answering themselves.)
- **Delay** — *Decide on Tuesday.* She promises nothing and lets the hearing settle what the heart will not: Augusta's rule honored, no answer in the room. (Consequence: every road stays open — but a pause is itself a signal, and the ton is fluent in pauses.)
> *(She folds the scrap into her reticule, next to the summons. The roads are priced, and the week, when it moves, will move on priced roads. The decision, when it comes, will be hers alone — the week's arithmetic, in her own hand.)*
