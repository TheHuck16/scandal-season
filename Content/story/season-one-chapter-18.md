## Chapter 18 — "Masks and Dominoes"

**Occasion:** The eve of Vauxhall — costumes, tokens, the household's planning; Thursday waits. **Preparation ritual:** the black silk domino — mystery as a garment. **Purse shelf:** the domino's silk; Vauxhall's boat.

**What changes for Rose:** She arms for Vauxhall the way her grandmother taught her — visibly, carefully, and at the exact moment it costs someone else the most. The tokens are weighed and chosen; the strategy is set; Octavia's sketchbook holds a truth in ink. Across town, Cecilia designs her trap and Rackham fails to buy the one thing in London that is not for sale.
