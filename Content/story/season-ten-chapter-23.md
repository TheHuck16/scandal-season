### L23.S9 · ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 · Rose (alone)
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE CODA'S LESSON (what the coda teaches: the unsparing, the just, or the keeping). The years deepen; decide the lesson. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The morning room, the proof read and sealed. The lesson sits on the table in its packet, and the years deepen around it like water around a stone.)*
> (T1 · the lesson, weighed) *the coda teaches — that is what codas are for — and the teaching comes in three weights: the unsparing, the terms' lesson; the just, the sharp-eyed's; the keeping, the held's. Rose turns them over the way she turns coins, feeling for the true ring. The unsparing taught her the seasons of arithmetic had a witness: the paper saw the ruthlessness and printed it, and the printing steadied her more than praise could have. She keeps the sentence about the arithmetic folded in her mind the way she keeps the proof folded in its packet.* — remembered: *the weighing.*
> (T2 · the just and the keeping, as lesson) *the just taught her the floor: the verdicts she could stand on when the ton's weather turned, the acquittal in the same size type as the accusation, and a floor, she has learned, is rarer than a ceiling. The keeping taught her the long game: the bound volumes, the gilt years, the thorns pressed flat, that what is printed true survives the century, and Bell built the paper knowing it. A floor you can stand on when the weather turns; a long game you can trust when the century tries to forget.* — remembered: *the surviving.*
> (T3 · the choosing) *she does not choose lightly. The coda's lesson will shape the coda's memory, and the memory will outlive them both, and she sits with the three weights until the light moves across the table and the chocolate goes cold again, the way it always does, and the packet sits on the table between the chocolate and the window, and the years deepen.* — remembered: *the sitting.*
> "Well." — Rose, to the packet, to Bell, to no one
> "Teach me, then. I am listening."
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — The coda's lesson:*
- **The unsparing** — *the terms, taught.* The lesson is unsparing — the Gazette's, the terms. (Consequence: the lesson is the terms' — but terms are law's, and the coda was the proprietor's.)
- **The just** — *the sharp-eyed, taught.* The lesson is just — the proprietor's, the sharp-eyed. (Consequence: the lesson is the sharp-eyed's — but sharp-eyeds are watched, and the coda was the ink's.)
- **The keeping** — *the held, taught.* The lesson is keeping — the coda's, the held. (Consequence: the lesson is the held's — but helds are kept, and the coda was the Gazette's.)
> *(The choice made — the means hers, the memory the coda's — and the lesson settles into the years the way ink settles into paper: permanent, and exactly where it was always going.)*
> *Three weights, one hand, and the choosing done the way Bell sets type: once, and to hold.*
