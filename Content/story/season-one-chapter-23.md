## Chapter 23 — "The War Tea Party"

**Occasion:** Augusta's war tea party — the council of war disguised as an afternoon tea. **Preparation ritual:** the war gown — command dressed as hospitality. **Purse shelf:** the campaign's purse — intelligence has its price, and the cakes must be excellent.

**What changes for Rose:** She runs the war from behind the tea table — chooses the campaign's face, assigns her four channels their weight, and wears the performance the afternoon requires — while Rackham's counter-move arrives over the seed-cake in a bonnet: the forged codicil, rumor or weapon.
