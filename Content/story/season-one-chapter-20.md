**Canon applied:** 1815. Rose Hartwell rebuilds Hartwell Park and earns her place; unhurried about marriage, honorable in method. Suitors honorable; routes grow through friendship. Villains sly, never overt. Mrs. Nance Bell is the Gazette's publicly named proprietor from the first edition — no unmasking plot anywhere; the paper judges and narrates. Fashion is a courtship mechanic with social consequence. Energy is the only throttle. Iron rule holds: time earns everything money can. Crowns (premium) / coins (soft).

**Occasion:** The masquerade at Vauxhall Pleasure Gardens — the Season's second tentpole. **Preparation ritual:** the domino assumed — the persona worn into the dark walks. **Purse shelf:** the supper box, the chairmen's fees, the mask's ribbon.

**What changes for Rose:** In the dark walks Julian confesses — he is the Network's coerced informant, and the coercion is his brother's debts — and Rose chooses what to do with a drowning man: trust, use, or distance. Lavinia weeps and the rival cracks; Cecilia's forged letter springs, and Rose sees her hand in it — the first proof. The masquerade ends with the thread in Rose's hand.

### L20.S13 · [C] · Rose (alone)
*Purpose: the tentpole ritual — the domino assumed in full: the persona worn into the dark walks.*
> **Occasion brief (published):** "Vauxhall After Dark — the masquerade. You will walk the dark walks tonight, masked, to hear what the ballroom forbids. The dress code: a domino that reads at twenty paces in lamplight and vanishes at five in darkness; the mask is Julian's black silk — the ribbon, the flower, are yours. > **The 3 directions:** *The Masked Stranger* (no allegiance, pure enigma — let no one read her) / *The Hartwell in Hiding* (the Park's colors under black silk — defiance, worn close) / *The Bait* (deliberately conspicuous — dress to draw the trap, and see who springs it).

### L20.S15 · [D] · Rose, Julian
*Purpose: THE CONFESSION — in the dark walks. "If I stop, my brother is ruined." The mended gloves, still mended.*
> *(The dark walks: gravel, black trees, the orchestra muffled to a rumor. He is waiting where the lamps fail — and when she comes, he takes her hands as if they are the only solid things in the garden.)*
> "I feed them items." — Julian (no preamble; the dark has stripped him of performance) "The *Gazette*. Little things — who danced with whom, who left with whom, whose bills are unpaid. I have done it for a year. If I stop —" (his voice breaks, mends, breaks again) "— my brother is ruined. Rackham holds his debts. *All* of them. I am the Network's — their *informant*, Rose. Coerced. Paid in my brother's solvency."
> *(He holds up his hands in what light there is: the mended gloves. Still mended. The neat, invisible mending.)*
> "A younger son's cage," — Rose (very quietly) "Not a rake's game."
> "You see it." — Julian "God help me, you *see* it. I won't name him — my brother. *Not yet.* That is a promise, not a refusal. Ask me for anything else. Anything."
*Animation: Custom (signature confession — the dark walks; bespoke two-shot staging, lamplight failing, the gloves as the visual thesis).*
