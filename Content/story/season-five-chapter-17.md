Outcomes converge: the game remembers the means (tone, relationship movement, Gazette flavor), never as a plot branch. ★ key-decision scenes carry the decision's 3 options as their turns; the Gazette sting is a single reading turn. **Canon applied:** 1815. The second installment stands at £5,000, assembling on its Tuesday at noon at the London house. Lucien de Valcourt never appears in person; only his money and paper act. Lady Agatha Drummond is answered only for now — never killed, exiled, or finally ruined; she returns, unbowed and unbroken. Mrs. Nance Bell judges and narrates, soft-voiced, sharp-eyed, blunt, never a mystery and never a prize. The gutter sheet is unnamed — never Pyke, Lantern, Pounce, or Lash. Energy is the only throttle. Iron rule holds: time earns everything money can. No Crown SKU touches the ritual.

**Occasion:** Tuesday — the choosing; the installment's noon; the hunt or the healing, the paper or the people. **Preparation ritual:** the Tuesday dress — the decision, worn with resolve. **Purse shelf:** the £5,000 assembling at noon in London; the hunt's costs, priced; the atelier's defense, the capital spent.

**What changes for Rose:** Rose chooses what her power is for — before the Assembly states it publicly. The hunt is permitted under Quill's rules but the people come first: she spends her capital on Élise openly, commissions the assembly gown from the bandaged hands, and takes the Park road at dawn to walk the human measure herself. Julian learns to trust her nerve; the routes divide and re-form at a higher altitude.
