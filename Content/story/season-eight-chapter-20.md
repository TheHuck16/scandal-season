## Occasion
London, Monday — the day after the sermons. The atelier's ordinary day, and the wound made physical. Cecilia works through others and never appears; the mother's money is never named.

## What changes
- The workroom's ordinary day: the crimson commission, the girls, the chalk and silk — the room made precious.
- The signs: a canceled order, a stare in the street, chalk crosses on the door.
- The warning answered: how Rose meets the signs.
- The escalation: the smashed window, the slashed silk, the message pinned to the door; Élise's defiance.
- The ritual: armor for the reckoning — Rose dresses to go to the workshop.
- The fire: the near-destruction; Élise's hands, shielding the girls.
- The choice under fire: the work, Élise, or the attackers.
- The smoke clears; the cost is counted.
