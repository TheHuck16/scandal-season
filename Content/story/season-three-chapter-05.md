## Chapter 5 — "The Street"

**Occasion:** The street — hawkers' cries at dawn; the town's ears, bought or earned. **Preparation ritual:** the hawker's livery considered; dress to be read by the street. **Purse shelf:** coins for hawkers' word; the campaign's purse is lean but aimed.

**What changes for Rose:** She takes the war to the street — buying hawkers' ears, not their cries — and learns the town's true allegiance is attention. The purse shows its depth.
