## Chapter 19 — "The Libel Action"

**Occasion:** The war goes to law — Hartwell v. the *Lantern*; the writ, served at the press. **Preparation ritual:** the service day — black, formal; the brief is the plaintiff, unbowed. **Purse shelf:** coins for the filing, the service, the counsel's brief; the action is paid in the open.

**What changes for Rose:** She sues in daylight — the writ answering Pyke's "Fraud's Last Performance" before the ink is dry. The street watches the *Lantern* stand alone. Lavinia delights. Octavia's miniatures enter the case as sworn evidence. And the Network, reading the writ, makes its first move — at Bell's door.
