## Occasion
London, the week of the wreck — Monday to Sunday. The Lash prints the wreck papers in full: the name of Octavia's late husband blackened, old ruin dressed as moral rot, to taint Rose by association. The studio is threatened — sitters cancel, a patron withdraws, the establishment cools. Rose stands by Octavia publicly, in the full view of the ton: the sittings, the doubled commission, the plain word. The romance deepens — devotion without surrender. And the print of the week is too coordinated for gossip: Rose begins to see the planting, and the war turns.

## What changes
- The wreck goes to full print: the rival presses blacken the late Mr. Wren's name — the ships, the ledgers, the drowning retold as fraud — the taint aimed past the widow at Rose.
- The studio under siege: sitters cancel, a patron withdraws, the artistic establishment cools its tone.
- Octavia's answer is precision — too much of it; Rose alone sees the cost.
- Rose's stand: seen at the studio daily, the portrait commission doubled and said aloud, the ton told plainly that she chooses her friends. The matrons' ledger grows; a dinner list closes.
- The heart holds: the sittings, the walk, the portrait begun — devotion without surrender, deepened.
- Julian, Laurent, and Henry stand as the wall — present, proven, unattacked this week.
- Bell's Gazette judges what it can prove — and the proving cuts both ways.
- The coordination shows: the sheets of the week are too neat for gossip. Rose sees the planting. The war turns from defense toward attack.
- The week's last hour: the proof — the wreck story was planted and paid for, and Rose knows the hand that paid.
