## Chapter 22 — "Debts Called Early"

**Occasion:** The debts called early; the household under siege; the opera — the first evening out after the calling. **Preparation ritual:** the armor gown — siege armor for the ton's inspection. **Purse shelf:** the siege economy — every coin a soldier.

**What changes for Rose:** The war stops being a campaign and becomes a siege — thirty days, counted like ammunition. She refuses to let Julian burn himself to light her way, takes the household's measure as a war council, and walks into the opera house splendid and unmoved while Rackham delivers her deadline to her box.
