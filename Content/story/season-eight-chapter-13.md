## Occasion
London, the honor's week — Monday to Sunday. The Lash prints the Horse Guards whispers; Henry is cut at a dinner and the mess goes cold; the question is put to him — step back from Rose and the whispers stop. He stands anyway and demands the inquiry sit, on the record. Rose stands beside him where the town can see. The inquiry clears him. The week's last hour brings the wreck papers.

## What changes
- The whispers become print: the rival presses revive the Horse Guards inquiry — Henry's name questioned for loving Rose.
- Society goes cold: he is cut at a dinner; the mess cools. The question is put — step back, and the whispers stop.
- Henry's answer: the inquiry must sit and clear him again — publicly, on the record, not in whispers.
- Rose's move: public loyalty — beside him at the inquiry's public sessions and in the ton's full view. The matrons' ammunition grows.
- Bell's Gazette: the judge, independent — prints what it can prove, no more, unbuyable under fire from both sides.
- Julian, Laurent, and Octavia each stand with Rose and Henry; the romances deepen by surviving.
- The inquiry sits, hears, and clears Henry — publicly vindicated.
- The week's last hour: the wreck papers — Octavia's late husband's name blackened, to taint Rose by association.
