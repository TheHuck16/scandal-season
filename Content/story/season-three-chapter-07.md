## Chapter 7 — "The First Libel's Face"

**Occasion:** A week of appearances under the libel's charges — every call a verdict. **Preparation ritual:** white, unadorned; the brief is the clean name (white roses, worn). **Purse shelf:** coins for the week's appearances; the campaign's purse is lean.

**What changes for Rose:** She faces each charge — fraud, dupes, swindle — and answers with a week of unimpeachable conduct. The ton begins to vote with flowers.
