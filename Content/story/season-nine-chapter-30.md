### L30.S4 · plot beat · Rose (alone) — "The Debt's Paying"
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE DEBT'S PAYING (how the debt is paid: how handsomely, how publicly). The paying waits; decide the manner of the payment. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The study, morning: the paying come due. The debt stands named — the books agree on the sum, the creditor's name is written fair, the draft waits in her pocket — and the settlement assembly will witness its discharge within the hour. The question is only the manner of it, and she turns the three ways of paying over the way she turns everything: slowly, and with the consequences laid out beside them like cards. Handsomely and publicly — the payment made before the whole assembly, generously, where all can see, the county carrying the story home. Handsomely and quietly — paid in full, without display, the dignity of the thing needing no witness, the books speaking for themselves. Exactly and publicly — paid to the sum before the assembly, precise to the shilling, and accounted, no man able to call it short. Each way pays the debt. Each way tells the ton a different story about the payer, and the ton will tell it for years.)*
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — The Debt's Paying:*
- **Handsomely and publicly** — *paid generously, before the whole assembly.* She pays the debt handsomely and publicly — before the whole assembly, the generosity entire. (Consequence: the paid is generous — the settlement is priced where the assembly witnesses the handsome, and the witnessing is the whole art; but the handsome is large, and the ton may read the large as the buying's.)

(Consequence: the paid is dignified — the settlement is priced where the handsome needs no witness, and the needing-none is the whole art; but the quiet is private, and the ton may read the private as the hiding's.)
- **Exactly and publicly** — *paid to the exact sum, before the assembly.* She pays the debt exactly and publicly — to the sum, before the assembly, the precision entire.
