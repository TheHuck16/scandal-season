*Purpose: the Dowager's verdict — what was built, and what it cost; Augusta at seventy-two, exact. (Turns: 8)*
> *(Augusta's morning room. The Dowager sits with the Park's completion laid out before her like a hand of cards — the mason's final account, the gardeners' roster, the inventory of the state rooms — and regards it all with the particular stillness of a woman who has waited a very long time to be unsurprised.)*

### L1.S4 · plot beat · Rose, Mr. Quill
*Purpose: the settlement's last paper — Quill closes the file; the house, hers beyond challenge. (Turns: 8)*
> *(Mr. Quill's chambers, by post and by visit both — the solicitor has come down to the Park himself, which he does for exactly two kinds of business: funerals and finalities.)*
> "The settlement is enrolled, Miss Hartwell." Quill lays the last paper on the library desk with something like ceremony. "The entail's true history is found, recorded, and beyond dispute. Mr. Hugh Hartwell's father's name stands cleared in the record —" he nods toward the window, toward the stone where the name is cut — "and in the stone. The mortgages are discharged. The title is —" the professional's pause — "unassailable."
> "Say it plainly, Mr. Quill."
> "The house is yours, miss. Beyond challenge. Forever is a word solicitors are not paid to use, so I will use it once and charge you nothing for it: forever."
> *(He stays for luncheon, which he never does, and eats Letitia's chicken pie with the air of a man who has closed a very long book. On the step he turns.)*
> "Miss Hartwell. In fifty-eight years of practice I have never seen a client finish what you have finished. Build something worthy of the paper it's written on." He bows. "That is my whole advice, and it is free."

### L1.S9 · ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 · Rose, Letitia
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE INVITATION (the ball's character: triumph, gratitude, or challenge). The guest list waits; decide what the night is for. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The library, afternoon: the guest list spread across the desk like a battlefield map, and Letitia with her pen poised. The first invitations go out tomorrow. The question is not who. The question is what the night is for — and the wording of the first cards will tell the ton before the ball does.)*
> "Triumph," Letitia says, "says: look what I built. Gratitude says: look what we built together. Challenge says: come and see if you can deny it." She taps the list. "The town will read the invitation the way Bell reads a leader — for the thesis. Decide, darling, before the ink does it for you."
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — The invitation:*
- **Triumph, openly declared** — *look what I built.* The cards announce a celebration of the Park's completion, plainly and proudly. (Consequence: the bold come curious and the timid come wary — the night has a spine, but spines invite breaking.)
- **Gratitude, widely shared** — *look what we built together.* The cards honor everyone who kept the house — servants named, tenants named, the town named. (Consequence: the household weeps and the ton is disarmed — but gratitude gives the dragon room to call it sentiment.)
- **Challenge, politely issued** — *come and see.* The cards are simple, confident, and impossible to refuse without admitting fear. (Consequence: the brave come and the cowards show themselves by staying — but a challenge is a dare, and dares get answered.)
> *(The game remembers the choice — and the means: triumph, gratitude, or challenge. The ball was always going to happen. The thesis is hers.)*
