# Scandal Season — Visual Design Lock v1
Stabilized September 27, 2026. This is the locked visual brief. Everything below is Beth-approved unless marked otherwise.

## 1. House character style (APPROVED)
- Beautiful adult young women, elegant and refined — never childlike, never cartoon-princess.
- Stylized 3D with a polished animated-film treatment. Natural, realistic head and body proportions — no oversized heads.
- No photorealism. Bright, airy, luminous presentation — never dark cinematic or glossy AI-game styling.
- Rose reference: `~/workspace/ts-spaces/ten-season-arc-review/assets/rose-ground-truth.webp` — preserve her face geometry, freckles, and crooked closed-lip half-smile in every render.

## 2. Outfit-design depth bar (standing rule)
EVERY outfit across EVERY scene gets the full treatment:
- Distinct embroidery/motif concept
- Distinct colorway
- Thread/embroidery options — the thread library keeps expanding ("options options options")
- Complete differentiated accessory set: gloves, earrings, necklace, hairpiece, hairstyle, carried item (fan, reticule, etc.)
- Complete look shown worn on Rose, never a bare dress form
- Reveals designed as acquisition assets suitable for screen recording

## 3. Thread library (ALL APPROVED)
Gold, silver, copper, ivory whitework, cut steel, black jet silk, polychrome.
Polychrome direction: pure silk embroidery with no metallic thread — APPROVED Sep 27, 2026: jewel, pastel, and rainbow wildflower variations.

## 4. Colorway rules
- Signature gown variant sets each live in their own colorway — the lineup can't all be red. The ruby Triumph trio stands only as the single-colorway demonstration.
- Full rainbow across chapters: jewel tones AND pastels. Purple must remain visibly represented. Do not cluster around green and blue.
- Everything unique chapter to chapter; reuse only when deliberate for the story.

## 5. Player choice vs canon (standing rule)
- Briefs and concepts are directional design, not hard locks. The player picks outfits via ritual micro-decisions.
- Briefs define the canon signature: what the story, the Gazette, and Season 10 remember — plus the atelier's vision.

## 6. Triumph Gown canon signatures (APPROVED)
- **Climbing Rose** — ruby, gold climbing-rose embroidery up the full skirt.
- **Hothouse Bloom** — sapphire, gold bouquet concentrated on bodice/neckline, deliberately plain skirt with three small gold buds near the hem, pearl-grey gloves, pearl earrings and necklace, braided coronet with pearl-and-gold comb, pearl-trimmed ivory reticule.
- **Walled Garden** — amethyst, gold trellis-lattice embroidery, garden roses at the hem, lavender gloves, gold chandelier earrings, gold rose pendant, chignon with gold rose hairpin, amethyst-and-gold bracelet, ivory lace fan.
- Establishes the pattern: 3 embroidery treatments × 3 colorways per signature gown set.

## 8. Standing presentation rules
- Visuals must never resemble generic AI-game art — light, bright, editorial.
- New characters appear first as illustrated plates, then as 3D renders.
- Loading scenes use fashion/botanical plate styling for chapter headers and character introductions.
- Historical target 1815, broad plausibility 1813–1820.
- Floriography appears in the 3D world.
- Asset families must remain repeatable and feasible for a solo AI-assisted builder.
