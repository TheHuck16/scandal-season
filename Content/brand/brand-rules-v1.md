# Scandal Season — Brand Rules v1
Established September 27, 2026. This is the brand foundation: every visual decision below is Beth-approved. The visual lock doc (`visual-design-lock-v1.md`) is the detailed design record; this document is the ruleset.

## 1. Identity

- **Brand name:** Scandal Season. Nothing else ever appears as the title.
- **Wordmark font ("Middle B"):** calligraphic swash capitals on a classical serif body, warm gold. Logo text is *only* "Scandal Season" — no subtitle, no tagline, no secondary copy. Ever.
- **App icon:** emerald cameo ground; Rose-like ivory relief (soft youthful features, rose in her hair — never a Greek statue); pearl border; blush roses; gold laurel; Middle B wordmark below the cameo.
- **Cameo motif:** the ivory cameo of the young woman's profile is the brand's signature emblem. It appears in the icon, the overlays, and the dressing frames.

## 2. Color

- **Primary metal:** warm gold.
- **Ground:** emerald green (cameo/icon ground).
- **Accent palette:** full rainbow across chapters — jewel tones AND pastels. Purple must remain visibly represented. Never cluster around green and blue.
- **Restoration language:** restored zones render in full jewel-tone color; unrestored zones in a sepia/dessaturated wash with dashed outlines. This convention is canon across the park board and all estate plates.

## 3. Wordmark dressings (the rotating set)

Four approved polychrome-embroidery dressings for the wordmark — the rotating cutscene-overlay set:
1. **Jewel rose** — climbing ruby roses, gold thread, ruby gems.
2. **Pastel pearl** — blush roses, lavender, pearls, amethyst.
3. **Wildflower gems** — rainbow wildflowers, mixed gemstones.
4. **Ivy emerald** — trailing ivy, jasmine, emerald jewels.

All four composited cutscene overlays approved: 1 (pearl-rose over orangery), 2 (sapphire-lavender over ballroom), 3 (emerald-ivy over wild garden), 4 (amethyst-lilac over folly).

## 4. Imagery style (house rules)

- Beautiful adult young women, elegant and refined — never childlike, never cartoon-princess, never oversized heads.
- Stylized 3D with a polished animated-film treatment. Natural, realistic proportions. No photorealism.
- Bright, airy, luminous presentation — never dark cinematic, never glossy generic AI-game art.
- Period-INSPIRED, not period-strict: target ~1815, broadly plausible 1813–1820.
- Rose reference (`~/workspace/ts-spaces/ten-season-arc-review/assets/rose-ground-truth.webp`): preserve her face geometry, freckle pattern, and crooked closed-lip half-smile in every render. Her hair: long, down, flowing, fantastic (updos available as styling).
- New characters debut as illustrated plates first, then as 3D renders.
- Every outfit reveal is shown worn on Rose, with hair, accessories, and styling — never a bare dress form. Reveals are built as acquisition assets, suitable for screen recording.

## 5. Fashion rules

- Fashion is a courtship mechanic, not decoration — every outfit choice carries social consequence.
- Outfit choice depth is the standing bar: motif × colorway × thread treatment × full accessory set (gloves, earrings, necklace, hairpiece, hairstyle, carried item).
- Signature gown variant sets each live in their own colorway — the lineup can't all be red.
- Thread library (all approved): gold, silver, copper, ivory whitework, cut steel, black silk, polychrome silk. Polychrome = pure silk embroidery, no metallic thread. The library keeps expanding.
- Floriography exists in the 3D world: flowers carry messages, warnings, declarations across all seasons.

## 6. Estate rules

- Hartwell House and grounds are the visual home of the brand: manor, arrival drive, ballroom, orangery, Emerald Parterre, wild garden, temple folly, kitchen garden, Sapphire Canal, stables, floriography rose garden.
- Geography is locked by the aerial survey plate: parterre off the south front; orangery near the house; folly a distance feature at the end of the lime avenue; ballroom → terrace → parterre axis; arrival forecourt = north front of the same house.
- Peacocks belong to the folly grounds. The parterre's tiered fountain is distinct from the merge board's small fountain piece. Kitchen-garden glasshouse ≠ orangery.
- Restored vs unrestored always follows the jewel/sepia convention (§2).

## 7. Currency and naming

- Premium currency: **Crowns**. Soft money: **coins**. Never "Gems," never "Rubies." Gemstones are reserved for side games.
- Placeholder copy in generated mockups (MERGE buttons, booster names, UI labels) is never canon.

## 8. Presentation rules (standing)

- Every image gets its own preview and voting cell; the voting cell sits directly beneath its image.
- Generation is not approval. "Needs changes" is not approval. Enthusiasm is not approval.
- Prose briefs and visual executions require separate approval.
- Never ask Beth to reapprove unchanged approved work.
- Visually consequential revisions require new review.
