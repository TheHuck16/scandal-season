# Merge Board — Item Chain Bible

**Date:** September 27, 2026 · **Status:** APPROVED by Beth (Board F) — chains are the launch set
**Rule:** Every item that appears on the merge board belongs to exactly one chain. The board's grid shows chain items and nothing else. Chain diagrams and the board always agree.

## Board rules

- The grid contains ONLY items from these five chains, at their correct stages.
- Every item shows its chain-family tag and a roman-numeral stage badge (I–V).
- Higher stages glow more; the final stage of each chain radiates.
- Merging three (or more) of the same item at the same stage produces the next stage of the SAME chain — never a random item.
- The chain codex (tap any item) shows its full chain line with the current stage highlighted.
- New chains unlock by season; the five above are the launch set.
