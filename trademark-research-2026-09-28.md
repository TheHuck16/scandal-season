## (b) Close variants

### "SCANDAL OF THE SEASON" / "THE SCANDAL OF THE SEASON"
**Result: NO federal trademark record found for either variant.**

However, both phrases are in active use as **book titles** (media-collision risk, not trademark risk):
1. *The Scandal of the Season* — Annie Burrows (Mills & Boon / Harlequin Books S.A.), published 2020 — romance.
2. *The Scandal of the Season* — Michelle Kenney (One More Chapter), audiobook released May 2025 — regency romance; reader reviews compare it to Bridgerton.
3. *The Scandal of the Season* — Sophie Gee (Scribner / Simon & Schuster), 2007 — historical novel set in 1711 London around a masquerade ball and Alexander Pope. Thematically closest to a Regency setting.
4. *Scandal of the Season* — Liana LeFey, audiobook copyright 2017 / audio edition 2022.

### ABC / Disney television series *Scandal*
- No federal trademark **registration** record for the TV series *Scandal* (Shondaland / ABC Studios; ABC 2012–2018, 7 seasons, 124 episodes) surfaced in any search performed. Credits carry only "Scandal © ABC Studios" copyright notices.
- ABC/Disney appear to rely on **common-law / copyright** rights rather than a registered word mark. Common-law use of "SCANDAL" for a high-profile TV drama is itself a collision surface (distinctive but not in classes 9/41 federal records as a registered mark).
