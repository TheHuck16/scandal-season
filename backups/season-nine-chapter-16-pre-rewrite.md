### L16.S5 · plot beat · Rose (alone) — "The Reading, Weighed"
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — HOW TO READ THEM (openly, with counsel only, or in secret). Choose the manner before the seals are broken. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The library at night. The Park lies dark beyond the glass and the candles burn low. The question will not wait: how does she read the codicils? She prices the roads at the window. Openly — seals broken before witnesses, the contents the town's by supper, courage the story. With counsel only — in chambers, Quill and Harrow at the table, the law's before the town's, prudence the story. In secret — alone, the contents hers before anyone's, privacy the story, the town learning only what she teaches it.)*
> *(She has priced them before — the ledger habit does not stop at money. The open road buys courage at the price of subtlety. The chambered road buys exactness at the price of leaks. The secret road buys knowledge at the price of looking afraid. The candles do not care which she chooses. The Park, beyond the glass, keeps its dark.)*
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — How to Read Them:*
- **Openly** — *the witnessed, the courageous.* She reads them openly — seals broken before witnesses, the contents the town's by supper. (Consequence: openness disarms the whisperers, and the town respects a defendant who does not flinch — but the town reads paper the way it reads people, badly, and the codicils' subtleties will be the first casualties.)
- **With counsel only** — *the chambered, the prudent.* She reads them with counsel only — in chambers, Quill and Harrow at the table, the law's before the town's. (Consequence: the reading is exact and the interpretation sound — but chambers leak the way houses settle, and the town will hear the shape of it before it hears the words.)
- **In secret** — *the private, the hers.* She reads them in secret — alone, the contents hers before anyone's. (Consequence: she learns the paper before the paper learns her — but secrecy is a costume the town recognizes, and the costume will be read as fear.)
> *(She chooses. The candles burn lower. Somewhere in the dark beyond the glass, the Park holds its breath the way the house holds everything — without comment.)*
