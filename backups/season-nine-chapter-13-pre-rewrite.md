### L13.S9 · plot beat · Rose (alone) — "The Answer, Owed"
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — THE ANSWER (accept the last de Varenne money, refuse it, or delay the answer). The offer is weighed; decide before the week decides for her. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The library, night: the offer weighed whole — the last de Varenne money, the family's final reserve, the final illusion laid at her feet without conditions. Augusta's daylight rule in the pocket. Quill's three shapes filed. Hugh's decency priced, the pity and all. The night demands the question plainly: what is the answer? She prices the roads the way Augusta taught her — once for the sum, once for the nerve.)*
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — The Answer:*
- **Accept** — *the taken, the weighed.* She takes the last de Varenne money — the rescue accepted, the family's final illusion laid on the Hartwell conscience. The defense is funded: counsel's fees, the paper hunt, the hearing's costs — the ledger's new line, roofed. But a taken rescue is a debt of the heart, and debts of the heart are not repaid in daylight, and the ton will price the taking in the town's grammar.
- **Refuse** — *the returned, the entire.* She sends it back whole — the pride kept, the defense unhelped, the illusion left in his keeping. The house stands on its own pride: nothing owed, nothing taken, the conscience clear. But pride does not pay counsel, and the week is expensive, and the hearing will not wait while pride counts its change.
- **Delay** — *the held, the unsaid.* She holds the answer — the week turns, the knowledge fills, the money waits. Decided from fuller knowledge, the answer ripens. But a held answer is a held man, and Laurent waits at four o'clocks that do not come, and a Frenchman held too long stops waiting and starts deciding.
> *(Taken, returned, or held — the night will sign one of them, and the carrying will be plain. The money was always going to be answered. The signature is hers.)*
