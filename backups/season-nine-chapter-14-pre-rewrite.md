### L14.S8 · plot beat · Rose (alone) — "How to Refuse It"
*Purpose: ★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — HOW TO REFUSE (refuse kindly, refuse firmly, or refuse by giving him no ground to stand on). The offering honored; decide the manner before the morning decides it. (Turns: 3 — the decision)*
> *(The library, night: the refusal formed entire — the commission unofferable-back, the resignation unsound three ways, the offering the point that must be honored. Augusta's counsel in the pocket, worn smooth by the evening's handling: kindly, or the kindest which is the firmest. The night demands its question, the one the morning will not wait for: how is it refused? She prices the roads the way she prices everything that matters — slowly, and twice. The kindly — refuse him gently, the friend's softness, the offering cradled even as it is declined, the manner Augusta named first. The firmly — refuse him entire, the Hartwell plainness, no softness for the whispers to misread, the kindest-as-firmest. The no-ground — refuse by giving him no ground to stand on, the case argued entire, the resignation made impossible before it is declined, Quill's three joints turned into a floor with no boards. Each road refuses the commission. Each seasons the refusal differently, and the seasoning is the signature.)*
*★ KEY DECISION 1/3 — How to Refuse:*
- **Kindly** — *the gentle, the cradled.* She refuses him kindly — the friend's softness, the offering cradled even as it is declined, the manner Augusta named and the heart's first choice. (Consequence: he is honored in the refusing, the offering kept warm — but kindness gives the whispers softness to misread, and the town misreads kindly the way it misreads everything gentle.)
- **Firmly** — *the entire, the plain.* She refuses him firmly — the Hartwell plainness, no softness for the whispers to misread, the kindest which is the firmest. (Consequence: nothing for the town to misread, the refusal clean as a parade ground — but firmness can bruise the offering, and the offering was the point that must not be bruised.)
- **No ground to stand on** — *the argued, the impossible.* She refuses by giving him no ground to stand on — the case argued entire, the resignation made impossible, then declined, Quill's counsel turned to architecture. (Consequence: he cannot resign what cannot be resigned, the floor gone before the fall — but an argued refusal leaves no room for the heart, and the heart made the offer and will feel the arguing.)
> *(The game remembers the choice — and the means: the kind refuser, the firm refuser, or the arguer. The commission was always going to be refused. The signature is hers, and the seasoning with it.)*
