## FINDINGS (ordered by severity)

### MAJOR

**1. Ambrose Pyke and the London Lantern appear in S2 (chapters 25–30) — storyboard says S3.**
Storyboard S2's hook is "the Network answers — and it answers in the gutter" — no Pyke. Storyboard S3: "Rival: Ambrose Pyke (sparing new face — one season; the Network's gutter weapon…)" — a NEW face, i.e., introduced in S3. The S2 chapters introduce him by name across six chapters:
- ch25:428: "Ambrose Pyke. The name arrived the way bad weather arrived..." — buyer of the *Weekly Mercury*; a full key decision — "*★ KEY DECISION 3/3 — THE PYKE PURCHASE*" (ch25:503), with three-option choice architecture (investigate / warn Lavinia / do nothing), ch25:504–505.
- ch26:22: Bell prints his name in the Gazette; ch26:196: "Texture — the Mercury's first edition under Pyke."
- ch28:535: "The gutter would answer Bath — Pyke's Lantern, the struggling sheet, the Network's coin behind the ink."
- ch29:388: "The *Lantern* — Pyke's little sheet — has been bought... The Network's coin, behind the ink"; ch29:403: "the libel — Pyke's Lantern, bought with the Network's coin, the first attack nearly set in type."
- ch30:176, 263, 274, 334–336, 688: the libel printed; the season's closing hook built on Pyke's Lantern.

Sharpest break — **Pyke appears on-page in ch29 S40**, violating the chapters' own "named, never seen" doctrine. ch25:465 establishes: *"She had never seen Ambrose Pyke. No one had described his face, his voice, his hands — only the hands' cleanliness... named, never seen. Sly. Never overt. That was the doctrine."* But ch29 S40 (`season-two-chapter-29.md:732–736`) stages him in the pressroom:
> "in a cheap pressroom off Fleet Street, Ambrose Pyke stood over his compositor's shoulder and read the first libel: set in type, inked, ready."
> *THE HARTWELL FRAUD — Bath's Ruin, London's Swindle, and the Suitors She Duped.*
> **"Run it," Pyke said.**
`scenes-season2.json` ch29 S40 lists participants `['Ambrose Pyke', 'the London Lantern']` — the only Pyke participant-listing in 1,200 scenes; Lucien has zero.
- **Violated:** storyboard S2/S3 rival economy (Pyke is S3's new face; "sparing new face — one season" is corrupted if he's named across six S2 chapters and shown in S2).
- **Assessment:** The gutter-answer hook itself is correctly placed per storyboard; the drift is the *naming and showing* of Pyke in S2. The named-only seeding (ch25–28) is at least defensible as hook-foreshadowing — **Beth should rule on that** — but the on-page pressroom appearance with dialogue in ch29 S40 breaks both the storyboard and the chapters' own ch25:465 doctrine and should be revised (the cliffhanger works with the compositor and the headline alone).
- Why not CRITICAL: it breaks no S2 tentpole — first blood, the hook, the price, Lucien's seeding all land as plotted.
- Note: S3 treats Pyke correctly as its one-season weapon and does not re-introduce him — the handoff is clean; only S2's early naming needs ruling.

**2. Drummond does not watch the vindication — plot-arc S3 closing beat missing.**
Plot arc (`ten-season-plot-arc.md:161`, S3 "Door opened"): *"And Drummond watches the vindication with the dragon's patience: the second installment, held."*
- **Chapters:** Lady Drummond appears exactly once, as early social texture — `season-three-chapters-1-3-pilot.md:605`: *"By Lady Drummond herself, who receives her exactly as usual…"* Absent from the vindication chapters (28–30); watchers are Fane, the Directresses, Bell, and the ton. JSON confirms: "Drummond" occurs only in Ch1 scenes (2 hits), zero in Ch2–30.
- **Why the chapters did this:** the beat map (`season-three-chapter-beats.md:28`) says *"Drummond: absent S3–S4."* The chapters faithfully follow the beat map — but the plot arc outranks it, and the storyboard is silent on Drummond in S3.
- **Complication:** the plot arc's own character ledger (`ten-season-plot-arc.md:375`) says *"S3–S4 — absent."* So the plot arc contradicts itself: ledger shorthand says absent, the S3 "Door opened" beat says she watches the vindication. The specific beat should normally win over the shorthand — **but this needs Beth's call**: either add one vindication-watcher beat for Drummond in Ch28–30, or rule that "absent" stands and strike line 161.
