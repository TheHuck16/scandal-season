## B. LOCKED SERIES RULES — all hold

- **Rival = the past itself, no new face** (beats). Mrs. Quick (the offer-bearer), Slade (the agent), and Pounce (gutter proprietor) function strictly as the hunter's "hired help" (plot arc l.251: "the past has hired help, and the help is closing in"). None is framed as a rival. ✓
- **Harrow = complication and eventual ally, never a rival, never romantic** (roster l.49). No romantic framing anywhere (ch19:91's hand-taking is protective doctrine: "No one touches the witness"). Settled as land agent or pensioner-historian, his choice honored (ch29 "What changes"). ✓
- **The Gazette-control war is DEAD; Bell is infrastructure, never contested, bought, or the prize** (beats engine note; roster l.31: "manipulable but unbuyable"). Bell's stings state it outright: "This paper's outrage is its own, and it is not for sale" (ch16:486); "The Gazette notes that some stories are bought. This paper has not bought this one" (ch17:498); "I printed it because it was true and it was news. Not for you. The Gazette doesn't print favors" (ch26:55). Rose never pays for print. ✓
- **Romance rule** (no fake courtship, no sisters' triangle; suitors honorable): Julian's fury leashed (ch16:107), Henry's duty-arithmetic (ch28:153), Octavia's counsel (ch18; ch28:83), Laurent stays (ch28:11). ✓
- **Drama rule** (real stakes, not mechanics): the pressure is a story about to print, a blackmail attempted, a living witness at risk — never a clock. ✓
- **Rose's anchor drives** (rebuild the estate; earn her place): the reclamation gives the garden to the estate's people (ch30:239); her place is tried in print and society throughout. ✓

## FINDINGS (ordered by severity)

### CRITICAL

**1. ch20 L20.S4 — "To the Vane family: three thousand pounds" in Octavia's ledger.**
Octavia's atonement-price reading lists the hunter's family as a £3,000 restitution creditor: *"To the de la Roche family: nine hundred pounds. To the de Brissac family: fourteen hundred pounds. To the Vane family: three thousand pounds."* (ch20:53)
- **Violated:** plot arc l.251 (Mrs. Vane's money funds the anti-Rose operation — she is the hunter, not a victim) and l.265 ("behind them a name: Mrs. Vane — surfaced, never seen"); the arc's victim list is the de Varennes and de Valcourts. Paying restitution to the family trying to buy and print the sin corrupts the choice's moral architecture.
- **Corroboration that it's stray:** the line appears exactly once in all 400 scenes. ch18's two creditor enumerations (ch18:172, 351) omit the Vanes; the atone-path accountings in ch26 (ch26:208, 337) and ch30 (ch30:189) omit them. The listed creditors also don't reconcile to the stated totals (listed sums = £10,000 vs. £8,800 now / £41,600 total), suggesting the line was added to a draft list without ledger discipline.
- **Fix:** delete "To the Vane family: three thousand pounds." from L20.S4. Do not replace it with another named family (the "never invent named entities" law); the remaining list already reads cleanly without it.

### MAJOR (pending placement — verify on delivery of unwritten chapters)

**2. The Act 2 London ball has no home.** Storyboard: "a London ball where the old scandal threatens to break" (beats ~l.195). No ball appears as an event anywhere in ch16–30. It must be placed in ch1–15 — the only remaining window (a week-1 Saturday ball fits before ch16's Thursday). If the ch1–15 delivery lacks it, that is a missing tentpole, not a deferrable beat.

**3. The Act 1 country house party has no home.** Storyboard: "a country house party at the half-restored Park, the estate as social stage for the first time" (beats ~l.195). Zero occurrences in ch16–30. Must be placed in ch1–15; verify on delivery.

### MINOR

**4. ch17:275 — "forty-one pairs of mended gloves."** ch16 establishes "forty pairs" three times (ch16:359, 432, 508), with the forty-first accounted for separately: "Forty wrists wore it tonight; forty-one, counting Harrow's spare glove in his pocket" (ch16:431). **Fix:** ch17:275 → "forty pairs."

**5. Invented émigré creditor surnames "de la Roche" (£900) and "de Brissac" (£1,400)** (ch18:172, 351; ch20:53). Not in the roster or storyboard. They are used consistently as ledger texture across ch18/ch20 (and the observed ch21), contradict nothing, and name no on-page character. **Fix:** either add to the roster as S7 creditor families or leave as texture — but do not expand them into characters without Beth's approval.

**6. Unnamed creditors "the widow" (£200) and "the captain" (£350)** (ch18:172; ch20:53). Consistent across chapters; acceptable as ledger texture. Note only — the "never invent named entities" law actually favors leaving them unnamed.
